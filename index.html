Brief Biography

Dr. Xiaofeng Cao is currently a Tenured Associate Professor at the School of Computer Science and Technology, Tongji University. His research focuses on fundamental problems in artificial intelligence, with an emphasis on learning theory, generalization, black-box machine learning and machine teaching, nonconvex optimization, and non-Euclidean geometry.

His recent research explores reliable generalization, trustworthy optimization, large language model reasoning, embodied intelligence, and scientific intelligence, with the goal of developing theoretical foundations for next-generation intelligent systems.

He received his PhD degree from the University of Technology Sydney (UTS), where he was affiliated with the Australian Artificial Intelligence Institute and supervised by Prof. Ivor W. Tsang. He has also conducted research collaborations with researchers at HKUST, A*STAR, and other leading institutions.

He has published nearly 100 research papers in leading journals and international conferences, including JMLR, IEEE TPAMI, ICML, and NeurIPS. His work has received several Best Paper Awards and other academic honors.

He is an IEEE Senior Member, NAAI Senior Research Fellow, and AIIA Scientist. He also serves as an Area Chair / Senior Program Committee member for major AI and machine learning conferences and as an editor for several international journals.

He is leading a research group working on machine learning theory, optimization, and emerging intelligent systems. He welcomes self-motivated PhD students, research assistants, visiting students, and master's students interested in these areas.
